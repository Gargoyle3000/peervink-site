Add To Cart

2025

Add To Cart is the first collaborative presentation by Peer Vink and Tom Putman. Their individual works are brought together within a shared installation, allowing images, objects and sculptural elements to operate as parts of the same environment.

The exhibition draws on the visual language of advertising, retail and display. Familiar symbols of desire, status and consumption remain attractive, while their accumulation gradually makes the installation feel unstable, excessive and slightly uncomfortable.

In collaboration with Tom Putman
Various materials and dimensions
Presented by Heejsteck#
Utrecht, 2025
