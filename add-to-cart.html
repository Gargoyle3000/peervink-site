Add To Cart is the first collaborative presentation by Peer Vink and Tom Putman. Built around a single artificial landscape, the installation takes its cue from the decorative rock formations, indoor fountains and planted features once commonly found in shopping centres and office buildings.

These fragments of nature soften spaces designed around work, circulation and consumption. The sculpture offers a brief sense of escape, while remaining visibly fabricated. What first appears as a break from the surrounding architecture gradually reveals itself as another constructed part of it.

In collaboration with Tom Putman
Various materials and dimensions
