The sculpture resembles a ritual prop from an imagined culture. Its stacked construction recalls the logic of a fountain or monument, but the materials and finish place it somewhere less familiar. The result suggests a cult ornament whose original function can no longer be clearly understood.

The work forms part of a broader exploration of how belief is given shape and authority. A flower-like form covered with metal studs crowns the object and functions as a cult icon, seductive in its finish but unsettling in its presence. Although no water flows through it, the sculpture still carries the upward movement and ceremonial force of a fountain.

Resin, Styrofoam, aluminium, studs, steel, wood, silk PLA, epoxy and toothpicks
180 × 40 × 40 cm

De Apotheker, Amsterdam, 2023
