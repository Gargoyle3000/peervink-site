No Fountains Without Water

2023

The sculpture resembles a ritual prop from an imagined culture. Its stacked construction borrows from fountains, monuments and ceremonial objects, while its symmetry, ornament and artificial materials suggest an artefact from an ancient civilisation translated through science fiction.

The sculpture forms part of a broader exploration of cults, mythic narratives and the visual languages through which belief is staged. A seductive, flower-like form covered with metal studs crowns the object. It operates as a cult icon: attractive and repellent at once. Although no water flows through it, the work retains the vertical movement, repetition and ceremonial presence of a fountain.

Resin, Styrofoam, aluminium, studs, steel, wood, silk PLA, epoxy and toothpicks
180 × 40 × 40 cm
Presented in Fountains Without Water
De Apotheker, Amsterdam, 2023
Later shown in Fountains Without Water as part of W139 Hosts
W139, Amsterdam, 2023
