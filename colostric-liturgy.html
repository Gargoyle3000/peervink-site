Colostric Liturgy

2024

Colostric Liturgy transforms the exhibition space into a contemporary crypt. Sculptures, architectural forms, light, water and found imagery combine to create an environment that feels simultaneously sacred, artificial and theatrical.

The installation brings old religious imagery into contact with technology, consumer culture and contemporary forms of spirituality. It considers the continuing desire for ritual, meaning and transformation within a hyper-digital world, while questioning the promise of immediate relief and the appeal of the quick fix.

In collaboration with Katharina Busl
Various materials and dimensions
Presented by Kunsthal Kloof
Utrecht, 2024
