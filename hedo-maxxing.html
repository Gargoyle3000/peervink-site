Hedo Maxxing brings together images drawn from internet culture, advertising, folklore and mythology. Their glossy surfaces retain the attraction of the images they borrow from, but the way they are combined gradually makes that attraction feel less stable.

Across the three works, familiar images are pushed towards a more inflated and theatrical version of themselves. In one of them, an exaggerated version of the artist appears on horseback. The image turns self-presentation into self-mythology: an inflated figure caught between aspiration, performance and parody. The same tension continues through the other works, while the epoxy fixes each scene like a relic and the aluminium frames and studs make the objects feel both protected and aggressive.
