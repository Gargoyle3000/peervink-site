Hedo Maxxing

2026

Hedo Maxxing brings together images from internet culture, advertising, folklore and mythology. Gloss, excess and familiar symbols are used as seductive surfaces, while the combination of the images gradually shifts toward discomfort.

At the centre is an exaggerated version of the artist on horseback. The image turns self-presentation into self-mythology: an inflated figure caught between aspiration, performance and parody. The epoxy fixes the scene like a relic, while the aluminium frame and studs give the object something protective as well as aggressive.
