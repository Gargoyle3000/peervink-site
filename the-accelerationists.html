The Accelerationists

2022

The Accelerationists was developed collectively by Bernardus Baldus, Wannes Missotten, Tom Putman and Peer Vink. Together, the artists created an environment somewhere between a showroom, a stage set and a ceremonial interior. Glossy surfaces, inexpensive glitter and ornamental excess turn consumption into an atmosphere: seductive, aspirational and deliberately artificial.

Each artist produced a sculpture in response to this shared visual language. Peer Vink’s Wellness Sculpture nr. 6 combines an enlarged display-like form with decorative and almost devotional details. The work moves between consumer object, wellness product and cult prop: attractive in its promise of transformation, yet unstable and oversaturated.

In collaboration with Bernardus Baldus , Wannes Missotten and Tom Putman
Peer Vink, Wellness Sculpture nr. 6
Styrofoam, ceramics, toothpicks, PUR foam, acrylic resin, epoxy and glass
100 × 80 × 45 cm
